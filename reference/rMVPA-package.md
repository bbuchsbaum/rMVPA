# rMVPA: Multivoxel Pattern Analysis in R

Multivariate pattern analysis of functional neuroimaging data. Provides
searchlight and region-of-interest decoding with cross-validation for
classification and regression, representational similarity analysis
(including cross-validated Mahalanobis 'crossnobis' distances), and
permutation-based inference with searchlight-size-adjusted null
distributions. Common classifiers and representational similarity models
are fitted by exact searchlight engines that share computation across
spheres. Methods follow Haxby et al. (2001)
[doi:10.1126/science.1063736](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1063736) ,
Kriegeskorte, Goebel and Bandettini (2006)
[doi:10.1073/pnas.0600244103](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0600244103) ,
Kriegeskorte, Mur and Bandettini (2008)
[doi:10.3389/neuro.06.004.2008](https://doi.org/10.3389/neuro.06.004.2008)
, Walther et al. (2016)
[doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2015.12.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2015.12.012)
, and Ahdesmaki and Strimmer (2010)
[doi:10.1214/09-AOAS277](https://doi.org/10.1214/09-AOAS277) .
